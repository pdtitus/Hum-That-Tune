## Rules (Current)

### Scoring
- Song Title = 2 points
- Artist = 1 point
- Year = 1 point (within ±1 year)

### Passes
- 10 rounds = 1 pass per team
- 20 rounds = 2 passes per team
- 40 rounds = 4 passes per team
- Using a pass immediately deals a new song.
- The team's turn continues.
